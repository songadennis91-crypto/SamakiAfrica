Multi-Factor Authentication Policy

SamakiAfrica adheres to RFC 6238 TOTP specifications. The 6-digit codes rotate every 30 seconds with cryptographic HMAC-SHA1 validation.

  • Zero SMS vulnerability (immune to SIM-swap attacks)
  • Fully offline verification (no network required on phone)
  • Emergency cryptographic single-use backup recovery codes
  • Session containment on suspicious login attempts
