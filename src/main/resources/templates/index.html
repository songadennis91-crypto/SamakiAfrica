Pan-African Marine & Freshwater Directory

Africa's Major Fishing Grounds

From the Great Lakes of the Rift Valley to the Atlantic upwelling currents and the coral reefs of the Indian Ocean, trade directly with certified fishers in every basin.

Lake Victoria Basin

East Africa

Shared by Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania. The continent's freshwater powerhouse providing high-volume Nile Perch, Tilapia, and Omena.

Key Ports: Mbita, Dunga (Kisumu), Mwanza, Ggaba, Jinja, Kasenyi
Explore Lake Victoria Catches →

Western Indian Ocean

East & Southern

Kenya, Tanzania, Mozambique, Madagascar, Somalia, Seychelles, and Mauritius. Pristine coral drop-offs rich in Yellowfin Tuna, Snapper, and Lobster.

Key Ports: Malindi, Mombasa, Dar es Salaam, Maputo, Victoria (Seychelles)
Explore Indian Ocean Catches →

Gulf of Guinea (Atlantic)

West Africa

Nigeria, Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Cameroon, Benin, Togo. Commercial coastal trawl fisheries renowned for Giant Tiger Prawns, Croaker, and Sardinella.

Key Ports: Victoria Island (Lagos), Tema, Abidjan, Douala, Cotonou
Explore Gulf of Guinea Catches →

Lake Tanganyika Basin

Central & East

Tanzania, DR Congo, Burundi, and Zambia. Earth's second deepest lake, world-famous for endemic freshwater sardines (Kapenta / Ndakala / Mukeke).

Key Ports: Kigoma, Kalemie, Bujumbura, Rumonge, Mpulungu
Explore Lake Tanganyika Catches →

Benguela Upwelling (Atlantic)

Southern Africa

South Africa, Namibia, and Angola. One of the planet's richest cold-water marine ecosystems producing deep-sea Cape Hake, Monkfish, and Kingklip.

Key Ports: Cape Town, Walvis Bay, Hout Bay, Lüderitz, Luanda, Lobito
Explore Benguela Catches →

Mediterranean & Red Sea

North Africa

Egypt, Morocco, Tunisia, Algeria, Sudan, and Eritrea. Premium European Sea Bass, Sea Bream, Bluefin Tuna, Red Sea Grouper, and Moroccan Sardines.

Key Ports: Alexandria, Agadir, Casablanca, Sfax, Port Sudan, Algiers
Explore Mediterranean & Red Sea Catches →

How SamakiAfrica Works

A digitized supply chain empowering artisanal fishers across 54 African countries while guaranteeing peak freshness for commercial hospitality and wholesale buyers.

1. Fishers Post Catch

Fishers list their catch either while heading out to water, upon net hauling, or upon docking with verified kg, species, country, and price.

2. Buyers Match & Order

Hotels, restaurants, and wholesalers browse live catches by country or water basin, negotiate direct, and place batch procurement orders.

3. Cold-Chain Fulfillment

Batches are collected in insulated ice containers or dispatched via refrigerated transit straight to destination cold rooms with verified provenance.
